Thank you for installing the Hybrid Chart!

Your release is named {{ .Release.Name }}.

To check the status of your deployment, run:

  kubectl get all -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }}

To view logs:

  # Manager logs
  kubectl logs -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} deployment/{{ include "hybrid.fullname" . }}-manager

  # Collector logs
  kubectl logs -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} deployment/{{ include "hybrid.fullname" . }}-collector

To uninstall the chart:

  helm uninstall {{ .Release.Name }}
